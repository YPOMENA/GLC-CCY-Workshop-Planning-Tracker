    How the days run

    • 8 hours of content against 9h30 of workshop blocks. The rest is buffer for the Global Board, global updates and overruns, including 30 minutes before the close on Day 2.
    • Global Board: first choice is held at the start of Day 1, second choice on Day 2 morning.
    • Seating: by chapter size on Day 1, and by region on Day 2 as far as numbers allow.
    • The hard hat marks a Chapter Commitment on every slide, worksheet and cue card.
    • Every slide carries its reference (such as Day 1 · slide 3), the presenter’s colour and its timing, so the cue cards still match if slides move.
    • Presenters switch often, and either Champion can jump in to add something.
    • Parking lot: anonymous, by Mentimeter QR. We review it at the end of Day 1 if there’s time; otherwise it carries over to Day 2.
    • Navigating the Challenges is on Day 2 only. The room ranks about ten common challenges, then works through the top three in three rounds. A scribe per table posts notes by QR, and everyone gets the export.
    • No overnight homework. Afterwards, participants get the hyperlinked deck instead of a separate resource index.
