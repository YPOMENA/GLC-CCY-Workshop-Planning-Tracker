      Mon 5 Oct 2026 · First planning call

      Alexandra (organiser), Delcie, Elizabeth, Nerissa, Paige · From the Zoom AI summary (shared by Alexandra) and Paige’s notes

      Theme: architecture. Structure, connections and a ladder, layered around the topic names we already use.

      Recap

      We planned the content and delivery approach for the workshop. The audience learns most through interactive methods such as case studies, so we won’t rely on pre-reads. Delcie shared what she learned last year: cover the essentials thoroughly, because most participants are new to chapter leadership. We keep the existing topic names and structure and add an architecture analogy. Guest speakers stay to a minimum, and Chapter Commitments gets a positive tone. Nerissa walked us through her management responsibilities spreadsheet, and Paige proposed one central space for all workshop materials.

      Agenda (sent 1 Oct)

      1. Apeiron in-person sessions
      2. Review of last year’s materials
      3. Key content to include
      4. Ideas and creative input
      5. Next steps

      Agreed

      • Keep Delcie’s four buckets: Foundation · Design & Execution · Building · Ribbon Cutting.
      • Bring Chapter Commitments back in, with a positive, supportive tone.
      • Frame every topic for a first-time Chapter Chair; cover it comprehensively.
      • The workshop is a transition moment: succession, handover, leave the chapter better than you found it.
      • Interactive over presentational, with light case studies (a different topic per table).
      • Casual: no costumes or elaborate activities.
      • Minimal guests. Chapter Commitments guests are a nice-to-have, set up by the Champions first.
      • Delcie promotes the Apeiron Program at GLC.

      Before and after the workshop

      • One pre-read: Chapter Commitment data, reviewed with the CM and outgoing CC in a transition meeting.
      • Pre-workshop survey: biggest concerns, most confident, anticipated challenge.
      • Hyperlinked follow-up PDF plus an index of tools and resources.
      • The RACI Excel is our live tool.
      • One shared, non-Google space for the RACI, presentation links, notes and schedule.
      • Materials final by the Christmas break; rehearse January to GLC.

      Actions

      • Delcie: review the content PDF and decide which topics to prioritise, which move to post-reads, and where case studies are most needed.
      • Delcie: walk through last year’s deck on 12 Oct and flag what works and what needs updating; promote Apeiron at GLC.
      • Elizabeth: review the content PDF for priority topics and areas to improve.
      • Nerissa: share the management to-do spreadsheet with the team for reference. Done: see the task list below
      • Paige: send the notes from this call and a high-level agenda for 12 Oct. Done: in this hub
      • Paige: create a shared, non-Google space for the RACI, presentation links and meeting notes. Done: this hub
      • Paige: draft notes and guidance for the Chapter Commitments section so it lands with a positive, supportive tone.
      • Paige: decline Alan Hepburn’s request to join the GLC session, as Delcie will cover the Apeiron content. Done

      Discussion highlights

      • Pre-reads. Delcie: people rarely read them, so how we deliver matters more than where the content sits. Discussion and examples land better than data on slides.
      • Case studies. Nerissa: case studies were the most engaging format in a workshop that scored nearly perfect ratings. We’ll review the existing ones and pick the most relevant.
      • Gaps. Chapter Commitments, a major focus last year, is missing from the current content table. Global updates were cut back significantly from last year.
      • Structure. Content sits in Delcie’s four buckets, with some topics spread across several sessions. Keep coverage comprehensive: this audience is new to chapter leadership.
      • Transition. Elizabeth: the workshop is the handover moment, so outgoing chairs should focus on succession planning and keeping the chapter running smoothly.
      • Naming. Nerissa: themed designs often lose the official topic names. Alexandra confirmed we keep them and add the architecture elements on top, following Elizabeth’s Montreal example.
      • Resources. Fewer pre-reads and stronger post-workshop resources: hyperlinked documents and a numbered index participants can note during the session.
      • Survey. A pre-workshop survey on concerns and challenges lets Delcie and Elizabeth adjust content before GLC.
      • Guests. Keep guest speakers to a minimum to avoid timing disruptions and technical issues.

      Owners to agree on 12 Oct

      • Pre-workshop survey
      • Index document & follow-up PDF
      • Case study selection
      • Chapter Commitments pre-read pack
      • Chapter Commitments guests
